# Job Search Preferences (EXAMPLE)

> Made-up example. Copy this to `private/preferences.md` and replace every value with your own. All cities, regions and companies below are fictional.

## Location
- On-site or hybrid: City A, City B, City C metro area
- Remote from Country Home: open to roles at companies based in Region 1 or Region 2, if the company and scope are genuinely strong. Happy to travel for work.
- Skip: remote roles based in Region 3 (time zone gap)
- Not relocating for now
- Home country (for remote roles): Country Home. A remote role must allow someone based in Country Home: remote Country Home, remote Region 1, or global remote / work from anywhere.
- Remote search regions: Country Home, Region 1, Region 2
- Skip remote roles that require Region 3 working hours, or that require living in or having work authorization for Country X or Country Y.

## Level
- Primary: Level 1 / Level 2 / Level 3
- At large companies (Big Company One, Big Company Two, etc.), also include Level 4 and Level 5, since titles there run lower than at startups
- Skip: entry-level and junior roles

### Lane 2: Adjacent roles (include only when the fit is strong)
- Adjacent role A, at company type 1

- Adjacent role C, best at company type 2

## Companies

### Dream companies
Acme Corp, Globex, Initech, Umbrella Labs

Any other company is fine if the role is a strong match.

## Skip
- Sales roles with revenue quotas
- Pure project or program management roles
- Roles that require relocating
- Remote roles based in Region 3

## How to score fit
- **Strong**: level and lane match, and the role asks for things the resume clearly shows (your top 3-5 strengths)
- **Medium**: level matches but the domain is new, or the role is an adjacent-lane role with partial overlap
- **Stretch**: needs things the resume doesn't show (e.g. managing much larger teams, very specialized domains). Still save it, but label it honestly.
