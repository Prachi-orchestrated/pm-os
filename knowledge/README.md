# Knowledge

Reusable notes and reference material that are useful across projects.

What belongs here:
- Frameworks (e.g. prioritisation, PRD templates, eval checklists)
- Research and market notes
- Learnings and retrospectives worth reusing

What does not belong here:
- Work tied to one project (put it in `projects/<project-name>/`)
- Anything personal or with contact details (put it in `private/`)
